# CIRCLE — Where creativity connects

Domain: circlecommunity.in · Operated by: Portfolix Enterprise Pvt Ltd (Kochi, India) with Portfolix.Media and Portfolix.Tech.

CIRCLE is a FREE community connecting creators, influencers, freelancers (photographers, videographers, script writers, editors, models), service providers (studio floors, podcast setups) and brands.

## Who uses it

- Creator / influencer: builds a profile, lists platforms + follower counts + rates, applies or bids for brand collabs, joins events, earns points/badges.
- Service provider: photographer, videographer, script writer, editor, model, studio floor, podcast setup, other. Lists services with "price from" and city.
- Brand: company profile, posts collab requirements (fixed budget, open bid, or barter), browses creators and their rates, hires.
- Admin (CIRCLE team): moderates, verifies, runs events, awards points/badges, manages partner perks.

One account can be a creator AND a service provider. Brand accounts are separate.

## What CIRCLE offers

1. Community: monthly meetups, community events, annual/periodic creator awards.
2. Learning (free): webinars, bootcamps — AI tools, earning from home, becoming a content creator, doing collabs.
3. Collab marketplace:
   - Fixed collab: brand posts requirement + budget (INR); creators apply; brand accepts one or more.
   - Open bid: brand posts requirement; creators submit a bid amount + pitch; brand picks.
   - Barter collab: product/service in exchange for content; no cash.
   - Brands see creators' rates (respecting each creator's visibility setting).
4. Services directory: find photographers, videographers, studios, podcast setups, script writers, models, etc.
5. Perks: partner discounts for creators (studios, gear, tools), negotiated by CIRCLE.
6. Recognition: points, badges, monthly leaderboards (creators and brands), awards.
7. Trust & safety: verification levels, reporting, removal of fake listings, fake collabs and fraud.
8. Agency services (paid, via Portfolix.Media / Portfolix.Tech), lead-capture only on the site:
   - For brands: influencer marketing, influencer negotiation, social media marketing, ad campaigns + ad management, ad video production, UGC content.
   - For creators: profile audit + optimisation, social media management (collabs, messages, posting, growth).
9. CIRCLE can act as middleman between brand and creator ("Managed collab" request form).

## v1 product rules (non-negotiable)

- Free to join. No paywalls.
- No payments processed in v1. Collab budgets are displayed; payment happens directly between parties. Show this clearly on every collab page.
- Collaborators must confirm they are 18+ at signup (checkbox + DOB). Under-18 users cannot apply to collabs.
- Every collab brief has "Ad disclosure required (#ad / #collab)" ON by default (ASCI influencer guidelines).
- Follower counts are self-reported until verified; UI must label them "Self-reported" vs "Verified".
- Never ask for or store social media passwords.

## Decisions already built in

1. No money moves through the platform in v1. Brands pay creators directly. CIRCLE records the agreement and the completion. In-platform payments come later (Part 9, deferred).
2. Collaborators must be 18+ in v1. Minors would need verifiable parental consent under India's DPDP Act.
3. Verification uses a code placed in the bio, not the Instagram API. The API requires Meta app review and only works with Business/Creator accounts.
4. Points come only from verified events (completed collabs, attended events, admin-approved tasks), never from raw activity counts. Otherwise the leaderboard gets farmed with spam.
5. Agency services (ads, social media management, audits, UGC) are lead-capture pages in v1, not software features. CIRCLE never stores anyone's social media passwords.

## Brand

- Colours: Obsidian #080808 (base bg), White #FFFFFF (text/logo), Electric Lime #C6FF00 (CTAs & highlights only, ~5–10% of any screen), Ice Silver #C7CBD1 (secondary text on dark), Deep Charcoal #171717 (cards/surfaces), Graphite #242424 (borders only).
- Contrast rules: Lime and Silver ONLY on dark backgrounds (on white they are 1.19:1 and 1.63:1 = unreadable). On white use Obsidian.
- Fonts (Google Fonts, OFL): Space Grotesk 500/600/700 for headings, Inter 400/500/600 for body. Scale ×1.25 from 16px: 13 / 16 / 20 / 25 / 31 / 39 / 49 / 61.
- Style: dark, editorial, geometric, high contrast. Thin circular outlines, open rings, connection nodes as decoration — sparingly. Rounded cards (16px), pill buttons. Uppercase for big statements, sentence case for body.
- Logo: use /public/brand/logo-white.png (full lockup) and /public/brand/emblem-white.png (the C only). Full logo only at ≥100px; emblem below that (favicon, avatars, mobile header). /public/brand/logo-obsidian.png is the same lockup for white backgrounds. Never retype the logo lettering in a font. Clear space is 2× the thickest part of the C stroke.
- Voice: confident, conversational, human. No hype, no fake numbers, no invented testimonials.
